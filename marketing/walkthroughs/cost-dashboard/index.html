See your cost & plan usage

3 steps

1

Click the $ icon next to Activity Log

Bottom of the workspaces sidebar. Or View → Cost & Tokens from the menu bar.

2

Read your plan usage

Top of the Summary tab: a "Plan usage" card showing your subscription tier and (for Max / Pro) live consumption of the 5-hour and 7-day windows. Pulled from Anthropic's oauth/usage endpoint.

3

Drill into sessions

The Sessions tab lists every recorded turn — provider, model, tokens, API cost, and a PR badge for PR-create turns. History shows a day-by-day cost bar chart for the last 30 active days.
